# Project Guidelines & Agent Instructions

## Design Guidelines
- **Avoid Aggressive / Extreme Overhauls**: Do NOT apply brutalist or overly raw design changes (such as jarring fonts, heavy border shadows, or heavy industrial styles) to the personal site unless explicitly requested by the user.
- **Maintain High Craft & Refinement**: Keep the design sleek, clean, modern, and professional (dark/light theme with subtle gradients, clean typography, balanced spacing, and high-quality image previews).

## Deployment & CI/CD Guidelines
- **GitHub Pages Workflow**: Use explicit GitHub Actions workflows (`.github/workflows/pages.yml`) for deployment to prevent `deployment_queued` timeouts.
